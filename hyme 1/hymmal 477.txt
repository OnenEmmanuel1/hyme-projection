1: O Jesus my shepherd Thou art
Let fear and distrust vanish
From the snares of lions and wolves
From devilish animal
His flock of sheep will He safe guard
Jesus will care for His own.

2: When my foes lay snares for my soul
Jesus hath said He is mine
For our redemption He has died 
O Jesus Lord what a love!
Victory is his disposition
Jesus is invisible.

3: He guides me through the way of life
Near the gently flowing stream
In the flourishing green pastures
Where there is poisonous leaves
There I hear the voice of Jesus
There He makes me to rejoice.

4: When I am nearing the graveyard
Let there be fear on my way
Dauntless and fearless I shall be
Because our shepherd is nigh
When I behold His rod and staff
I know that His sheep rejoice.
