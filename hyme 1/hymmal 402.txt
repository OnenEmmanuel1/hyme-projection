1: By faith, I crave to walk with God,
The end of my journey is heaven
Thy rod and staff, they comfort me
The Way Thou has pav'd is the best.

2: I walk amidst great wilderness 
Where many' souls have lost their way
But He that leads me on the way 
Will not allow my-loot to stray.

3: Walk amidst dangers and snares…
Earth and heH against me arrayed
Through Thy great power I have gone through
By faith alone, I have vict'ry.

4: I pity. Those who are boasting 
Of fleeting pleasures of this life
Oh Jesus, let me-walk with Thee
My Lord my Saviour and my friend
