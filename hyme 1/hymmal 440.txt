1: Speed Thy servants,
Saviour, speed them;
Thou art Lord of winds and waves;
They are bound,
but thou has freed them,
Now they go free the saves,
Be thou with them (2ce)

2: Friends, and home,
and all forsaking
Lord they go our they command;
As their stay thy promise talking
While they travel sea and land;
O be with them!
Lead them safely by the hand.

3: Where no fruit appear to cheer them,
And they seem to toil in vain,
Then in mercy, Lord draw near them,
Then their sinking hopes sustain;
Thus supported
Let their zeal revive again.

4: In the midst of opposition,
Let them trust, o Lord, in thee,
When success attends their mission,
Let thy servants humbler be;
Never leave them
Till thy face in heaven thy see;

5: There to reap in joy for ever
Fruits that grows from seed here sown,
There to be with Him who never
Ceases to preserve His own,
And with gladness
Give the praise to Him alone.
