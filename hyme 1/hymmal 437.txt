1: Rescue the perishing 
Care for the dying
Snatch them in pity
from sin and the grave;
Weep o'er the erring one,
Lift up the fallen
Tell them of Jesus
the Mighty to save

Rescue the perishing 
Care for the dying 
Jesus is merciful, 
Jesus will save.

2: Tho' they are slighting Him 
Still He is waiting,
Waiting the penitent
child to receive,
Plead with them earnestly,
Plead with them gently:
He will forgive
if only they believe.

3: Rescue the perishing, 
Duty demands it;
Strength for thy labour
the Lord will provide;
Back to the narrow way
Patiently win them
Tell the poor wand'rer a
Saviour has died.
