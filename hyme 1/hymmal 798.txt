

1. Thy love has spared our lives today,
Though we lay-down, spent and worn,
Guard us in the stillness of night,
Us no pow'rs of darkness harm;
Jesus be our guardian ever,
It is sweet to trust in Thee.

2. Pilgrims in this world and strangers
Dwelling in the midst of foes;
Keep us and dear ones from danger,
On thy wings in comfort sleep;
And when life's distress is over,
May our souls repose in Thee.
