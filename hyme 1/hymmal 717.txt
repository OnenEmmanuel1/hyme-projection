1: Come, Thou long-expected Jesus,
Born to set Thy people free;
To release us from sin's terror,
And our rest to find in Thee.

2: Thou art Israel's consolation, 
And the hope of earth, Thou art 
Thou art every nation desire 
Filling longing hearts with joy.

3: Thou art born to deliver us 
Thou art King, but born as child
Eternally, to reign in us
Thy gracious Kingdom to bring.

4: Let Thy own eternal Spirit 
Govern in our hearts alone 
By Thy all sufficient merit
Take us to Thy glorious throne.
