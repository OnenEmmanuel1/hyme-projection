1: By whom, Lord, shall Jacob arise?
His friends are very few;
What is surprising is this that
They are not'n one accord.

2: By whom, Lord, shall Jacob arise?
His enemies are cruel
They joy as if they have riumphed
And said, He is finished.

3: By whom, Lord, shall Jacob arise?
Can anybody say?
The dried up branch there on the ground
Can it bloom anymore?

4: O Lord my God, it is Thy work 
No one else can do it;
Descend like the dew on Jacob
And Jacob shall arise.
