1: If sinners join their hands,
And rise against the Lord,
Rebel against the Christ,
And despise the great King;
The world may mock,
Satan may threat,
It's all deceipts,
It's all in vain.

2: Our risen Saviour reigns,
On Zion's holy hill,
It is the Lord's decree
Upholds and keeps His Son
From the cold tomb
He bids Him rise
And to ascend
That He might save.

3: O serve the Lord in fear,
Revere His blest command
With gladness come to Him
In awe stand before Him
Bow down the knee
And prostrate fall
Tell of His might
Kiss thou the Son.
