1: Jesus gave His life a ransom
yonder on Calvary,
On Mount Calvary, cruel Calvary;
Paved the way by blood that
we might win a bright shining crown,
Praise His holy name.

Praise the Lord,
salvation has been bro't down,
Go and shout and tell it the world around,
Tell it today, tell it today,
Preach the word of God that we might win a crown;
Tell the lost, salvation is full and free,
Spread the news all over the land and sea,
Tell it afar, tell it afar,
Praise the Lord, salvation
has been brought down.

2: All alone without a friend
He suffered to pay it all,
Yes, He paid it all, Jesus paid it all;
In His blessed promises
sweet victory can be found,
Praise His holy name.

3: There's a blessed home prepared
'way over in glory land,
In bright glory land, blessed glory land;
I have trusted in His love and 
now I am heaven bound 
Praise His holy name.
