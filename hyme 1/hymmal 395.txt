1: It may be in the valley,
Where countless dangers hide; 
It may be in the sunshine,
That I in peace abide:
But this one thing I know,
If it be dark or fair,
If Jesus is with me,
I 'II go anywhere !

If Jesus goes with me, 
I'll go .... anywhere! 
'Tis heaven to me, 
Where'er I may be 
If He is there!
I count it a privilege here...
His cross to bear;
If Jesus goes with me,
I 'II go anywhere!

2: It may be I must carry
the blessed word of life
Across the burning deserts
to those in sinful strife;
And though it be my lot
to bear my colours there,
If Jesus goes with me,
I 'II go anywhere!

3: But if it be my portion
to bear my cross at home,
While others bear their
burdens' beyond the billow's foam,
I 'II prove my faith in Him -
confess His judgments fair,
And if He stay with me,
I 'II stay anywhere!

4: It is not mine to question 
the judgments of my Lord, 
It is but mine to follow
the leadings of His Word; 
But If I go or stay,
or whether here or there,
I 'II be with my Saviour,
content anywhere!
