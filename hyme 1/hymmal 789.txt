1: O God from all generations,
Thou art our rest and shelter too
Ere the world and this earth were made
Thy love is high above the sky.

2: Thou reigneth ere the world exist
Before the creation of men,
Thy Kingdom shall for ever be
Until that time the world shall end.

3: Death sweeps us away like river
The world exist in vanity
Like rays of sun in the morning
Cut and wither'd in the evening.

4: O let us know our wickedness,
And further our days here on earth
Until we are prepar'd for death
By sanctification of grace.
