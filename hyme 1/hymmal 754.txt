1: He's asleep Jesus said
Of Lazarus His friend
He who was dead, and was buried
And laid in His own grave.

2: He who died for four days
Jesus said, he's asleep
He's the resurrection and life
For all those that trust Him.

3: Jesus' friends never die
But their bodies conceal
Away from worm, below the ground
Now to fly in the sky.

4: Sleep on thou beloved
Jesus is by your side
Be not afraid, for He promised
Resurrection to us.

5: His voice will soon be heard,
From thy now resting place
His sweet voice will now calion thee
Into resurrection.

6: Let's not be sorrowful
Like one that has no hope
We bid thee farewell dear belov'd
Sleep on and take thy rest.

7: And we also shall sleep
At the time of our call
O that we at dawn meet again
At the feet of our Lord.
