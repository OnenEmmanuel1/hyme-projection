1: Awake my soul and rise with joy
Oh sing praises to thy savior
His grace and glory opens my song 
How great is his love and kindness

2: He knows I am lost when I fall 
Yet his love to me is complete
He have saved me from afflictions
How great is his love and kindness 

3: Darts from my foes at me are hurled
World and satan stand in my way
Graciously He had led me through 
How great is his love and kindness.

4: This weak heart of mine is always 
Transgressing the laws of the Lord 
The more I tend to forget Him 
His endless love will fall.
