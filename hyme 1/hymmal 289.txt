1: I'm revived, my soul is at rest
When I know my Saviour
All my sins, He has forgiven.
Glory be to the Lord. 

2: One day we shall reach heav'n above.
And all problems shall cease
No more sorrow to molest us
We shall rejoice always. 

3: E'en tho' I inherit the world
It's vanity to me
The incorruptible treasures
Awaits me in the high.
